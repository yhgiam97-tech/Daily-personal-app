# Daily Brief

A phone-first morning brief that installs to your home screen:

- **What to wear:** today's first-choice colours from your 衣服颜色 day-star chart, plus the 2nd and 3rd choices and tomorrow's colour
- **Top 3:** your priorities for the day, kept only on your phone
- **US markets:** index closes, your watchlist, what moved and what's coming up, refreshed every morning
- **Today's lesson:** a 20–30 minute AI & automation task, with a streak and weekly progress

Things to tap:
- **Week strip:** shows the next 14 days. Tap a day, or swipe the date sideways, to preview its colours.
- **Colour circles:** tap one to see its name.
- **Market rows:** tap a row to expand it for the day's move and a chart link.
- **Colours tab:** a month calendar of colour dots, each day's three choices, and how your chart works.
- **Lessons tab:** every lesson with its status. Open one to read it and mark it done.
- **Navigation:** pull down to refresh, and use the back gesture on both iPhone and Android.

## Sync
Top 3 and lesson ticks can sync between your iPhone and Fold through a private repo. Tap **Sync** at the bottom of Today and follow the one-time steps there. Nothing personal is stored in this public repo.

## Updating it
Ask Claude in chat, for example "here's next month's colour chart", "add MSFT to my watchlist" or "give me weeks 5–8". Claude edits the files in `data/` and pushes, and the app picks up the change the next time you open it.

## Install
- **iPhone:** open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open the site in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
